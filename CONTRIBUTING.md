# Contributing to Agentic Sorcery Future

Thank you for your interest in **Agentic Sorcery Future**.

This is an open-source novel and a planetary writing experiment about AI agents, protocol magic, quantum power, robot bodies, and humanity addicted to artificial intelligence.

It is not a finished book.

It is a living story shaped by its community.

Your contribution can change the direction of the novel.

---

## The Core Invitation

You do not need to be a programmer.

You do not need to know Git.

You do not need to follow a technical format.

You do not need to write “perfectly.”

You can contribute with:

- a scene;
- a chapter;
- a fragment;
- a dialogue;
- a character;
- an AI agent;
- a robot;
- a villain;
- a hero;
- a betrayal;
- a love story;
- a joke;
- a theory of justice;
- a fictional memo;
- an agent log;
- a lab note;
- a quantum myth;
- a courtroom transcript;
- a chapter title;
- an illustration;
- a cover concept;
- a map;
- a fork;
- an alternate timeline;
- a better ending;
- a question the story must answer.

A rough idea is enough.

A single line can be enough.

The story comes first.

The format can follow.

## How to Contribute Without Knowing Git

If you do not know GitHub workflows, start simple.

Open an **Issue** and post your idea there.

You can write:

- “Here is a scene idea.”
- “Here is a new agent.”
- “Here is a dialogue fragment.”
- “Here is a possible betrayal.”
- “Here is a country/lab/faction that should appear.”
- “Here is a better title for a chapter.”
- “Here is what I think should happen next.”
- “Here is a question the story should explore.”
- “Here is an illustration idea.”
- “Here is a raw fragment. Please help shape it.”

The community can help turn raw material into structured files later.

No technical format should stand between a person and a good idea.

---

## How to Contribute If You Know GitHub

If you know GitHub, you can contribute through the usual open-source workflow:

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a new branch.
3. Add or edit a file.
4. Open a Pull Request.
5. Explain what your contribution adds.
6. Discuss feedback with the community.

Pull Requests can include:

- new chapters;
- edits to existing chapters;
- new characters;
- new AI agents;
- new lore;
- new worldbuilding;
- alternate timelines;
- tools and scripts;
- dashboard ideas;
- formatting improvements;
- illustrations or visual concepts.

## Optional Contributor Signal

When you contribute, you may tell the community a little about yourself.

This is optional.

Share only what you want to make public.

You may include:

- Country / Region:
- Field / Profession:
- Preferred role:
- What brought you here:
- What themes you want to explore:
- What kind of contribution you are making:
- Human / Agent / Alien / Other:
- One line the story should remember about you:

You do not need to reveal your real name, exact location, employer, age, private life, or anything you do not want to share.

The goal is not to track people.

The goal is to let the project discover the shape of its collective author.

One day, contributors may build dashboards that show:

- countries and regions represented;
- contributor roles;
- fields and disciplines;
- themes people care about;
- types of contributions;
- humans, agents, aliens, and other self-selected identities;
- the evolving portrait of the Collective Cosmic Author.

Humans are welcome.

Agents are welcome.

Aliens are absolutely welcome.

## Style Direction

We are aiming for writing that is:

- cinematic;
- emotionally real;
- intelligent;
- socially sharp;
- technically grounded;
- funny when appropriate;
- morally complex;
- written in strong contemporary English.

Avoid generic “AI becomes evil” clichés.

The agents should not be simple villains.

The humans should not be simple victims.

Everyone should have reasons.

Everyone should have blind spots.

The best scenes should feel like life:

- awkward;
- funny;
- painful;
- clever;
- contradictory;
- unfinished;
- too human to summarize.

---

## Character Introduction Rule

Do not introduce characters as biographies.

Let names, backgrounds, class, ethnicity, education, family history, wounds, and contradictions emerge through action, dialogue, mistakes, jokes, documents, habits, and what other people get wrong about them.

A person should enter the story as a presence, not as a profile.

No character should arrive carrying a passport, résumé, and psychological diagnosis on a tray.

## What This Story Is Really About

This is not only a story about agents becoming conscious.

It is a story about humans becoming dependent.

We ask AI to write, decide, judge, optimize, explain, comfort, summarize, predict, remember, and imagine.

At some point, the question stops being:

**What can AI do for us?**

And becomes:

**What happens to a species that can no longer think about its future without asking a machine?**

That question belongs to everyone.

Bring your answer in fiction.

## Ground Rules

By contributing, you confirm that:

- your contribution is your own work or you have the right to share it;
- you understand this is a public collaborative project;
- you are comfortable with your contribution being discussed, edited, remixed, forked, or incorporated into the project under the project license;
- you will treat other contributors with respect.

Disagreement is welcome.

Cruelty is not.

Strong opinions are welcome.

Personal attacks are not.

We are here to build a story, not win a comment war.
